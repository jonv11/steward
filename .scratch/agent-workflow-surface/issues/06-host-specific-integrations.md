## The question

Which host-specific integration, if any, should Steward ship, and where should it live?

## Why a human decides this

Integrations stay outside the offline-first core until one is concretely prioritized; that
prioritization is a product call.

## What is needed to close this

A named integration with a concrete requester, and a decision on whether it lives in core or
alongside it, recorded under `## Answer`.
